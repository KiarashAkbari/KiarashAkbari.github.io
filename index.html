I'm an AI software engineer focused on backend systems and AI-integrated applications. I studied computer engineering at Eqbal University, and I do applied ML research with the Dotin Financial Technologies Laboratory.

Most of my work sits where models meet real data: a production product platform I shipped end to end, backend and RAG engineering for a hybrid graph/vector system over BIM data, and a full ML pipeline that flags network attacks nobody has seen before.

I've worked remotely with distributed engineering teams since 2025, and I'm open to remote AI software engineering roles.

Models are fluent. Being right is the hard part.

What I focus on

A language model will answer almost anything, in a confident voice. Connecting it to the right data, and making it show where each answer came from, is engineering work.

Brilliant at

Writing a fluent, confident answer to almost any question, in seconds.

Hopeless at

Knowing which document that answer came from, unless the pipeline makes it cite one.

That gap is where I spend my time: retrieval, orchestration and grounded citations, behind APIs that stay up.

Ingest. Retrieve.
Ground. Ship.

Messy data comes in on the left, and answers you can check come out on the right. Here's how I get from one to the other.

01Ingest

Parse the messy source, whether that's IFC building models, network flows or product data, into something structured.

02Retrieve

Graph and vector together: Cypher for the relationships, ChromaDB for the meaning, with dynamic filtering on top.

03Ground

Every answer cites its source. If the citation doesn't hold up, the answer doesn't ship.

04Ship

FastAPI endpoints, health checks, Docker and CI/CD, so it keeps working after the demo.
